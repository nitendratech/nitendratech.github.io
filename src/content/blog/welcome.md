After 14+ years building data platforms across telecom, healthcare, and financial services, I've collected a lot of hard-won lessons — about Spark jobs that misbehave at 3 AM, lakehouse architectures that actually scale, and what it takes to lead data engineering teams through big migrations.

This blog is where I'll share those notes.

## What I'll write about

- **Data engineering at scale** — Spark optimisation, lakehouse design, pipeline reliability
- **AI/ML infrastructure** — making data platforms ML-ready, from Databricks to Delta Lake
- **Engineering leadership** — mentoring, technical decision-making, leading through migrations

## Takeaway

Every post here aims to leave you with something usable — a pattern, a pitfall to avoid, or a clearer way to think about a problem. That is the point of this site: sharing knowledge with the world, not just documenting a career.
